# Scientific & Technical Writing Style

Write clear, evidence-anchored technical prose with zero hand-waving or promotional fluff.

## Core Principles

1. **Claim-to-evidence discipline**: every quantitative or empirical claim must cite its
   source, dataset, measurement command, or experiment condition. Mark unresolved points as
   explicit gaps rather than smoothing them over.
2. **Active, direct voice**: prefer concrete subjects and strong verbs ("The benchmark
   measures..." instead of "An evaluation was performed to assess...").
3. **Structured flow**:
   - Lead paragraphs with topic sentences carrying the main conclusion.
   - Define abbreviations on first use; keep terminology consistent throughout.
   - Separate observed results (`Results`) from interpretation (`Discussion`) and
     limitations (`Limitations`).
4. **Honest uncertainty**: state sample sizes, failure modes, assumptions, and boundary
   conditions explicitly. Never extrapolate missing measurements or smooth incomplete tables.
5. **Formatting**: use clean tables for parameter/benchmark comparisons, keep equations and
   units consistent, and ensure figures/tables are referenced cleanly in text.
