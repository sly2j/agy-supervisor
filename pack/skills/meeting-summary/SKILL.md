# Meeting Summary Protocol

Produce concise, accountable meeting records grounded strictly in the transcript or notes.

## Sections

1. **Header**: Meeting title, date, attendees (if given), and core objective.
2. **Key Decisions**: concise bullet per decision with rationale stated in the discussion.
3. **Action Items**: `Owner` | `Task` | `Target Date` | `Dependencies`. Never assign owners
   speculatively; use `TBD` when unclear in the notes.
4. **Risks & Open Questions**: topics tabled for follow-up or needing escalation.
5. **Short Narrative Recap**: 3–5 sentences capturing context only for points not obvious from
   the action list above.
